# FIN-01 vintage extension — protocol (2026-11-27)

Freeze before retrieving the new ZIPs or scoring new periods. Keep all five rules and hyperparameters unchanged; joint regime is a secondary candidate, not independently selected. Official archive catalogue https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/Data_Library/f-f_factors_archive.html links July 2024, July 2025 and August 2026 cuts. The July cut was released in August. The 2026 cut is dated by its catalogue link (September 2026 update). The switch from FIZ to CIZ began January 2025 and changes return compounding and dividend timing; see https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/Ken.french/data_library_202412_archive.html . Never splice FIZ/CIZ silently. No raw returns redistributed.

Three comparisons:
1. Original December 2024 FIZ vs August 2026 CIZ on overlapping 1182 months; count revised returns and changed <= -5% labels, max/mean absolute differences, 1990–2024 label changes; same frozen models re-evaluated on CIZ as a revision sensitivity, not new independent evidence.
2. On August 2026 CIZ only, evaluate Jan 2025–Aug 2026 (20 monthly targets) using frozen rules and t-2 training/feature cutoff; modern vintage reconstruction, not authentic as-of forecast. Keep global and recent reference. No tuning.
3. Two source-separated single-month reconstructions: July 2024 vintage for September 2024, scored with July 2025 vintage; July 2025 vintage for September 2025, scored with August 2026 vintage. Each July source was released in August preceding target under archive documentation; verify coverage, hashes and links. These are retrospective reconstructions from dated sources, not forecasts issued at the time. Report probabilities and binary outcomes as aggregate scores, no raw monthly returns. Two months cannot establish skill; first crosses FIZ/CIZ. Do not select target months using outcomes.

All monthly data must have exact July 1926 start, contiguous dates and advertised last month. Source ZIP hashes and sizes recorded. Require frozen FIZ source hash. Model outputs aggregate only. No new forecasts, no trades, no claims of economic utility. The current mutable source will be separately admitted before any December 2026 issue (requires complete October 2026 inputs with observed pre-issue availability). The next issue must predefine its endpoint vintage and publication receipt.
