## Estimand and locked design
Fixed feature window August 1–October 31 2026, reconstructed from three SHA-pinned monthly sources in PR162; all 196 country IDs in existing universe. Forecast target: total GED best fatalities from type 1 state-based organized violence events with date_prec 1 or 2 and event dates wholly contained in the target window. Windows inclusive: 2026-12-01 to 2026-12-30 (30 days), and 2026-12-01 to 2027-02-28 (90 days). Thresholds 1,5,10,25; only countries with feature-window best below the corresponding threshold are eligible. This is threshold reactivation, not onset of war and not a forecast of individual attacks. Missing event records are not proof of peace. Excluded country rows have null probabilities, never zero.
Training years fixed to 2021/22/23 only, using the original frozen monthly training panel and first annual GED labels for May windows, matching the conservative two-year embargo in prior model code. 2025 remains an historical evaluation year, not reused to fit the pilot. All six previously defined models, fixed k=20 shrinkage and untrained equal-weight pools. Primary probabilistic comparison: pool_three vs global at T10/H90; primary ranking comparison: country_history vs random at T10/H90 k=5. These choices are informed by retrospective results and not blind. All eight cells, six models and budgets 5/10/20 remain mandatory secondary outcomes, no tuning. Tie-aware expected hits using frozen serialized probabilities, no arbitrary country ordering. Forecast artifact contains country IDs and probabilities only, no event-level data.
## Publication and resolution
Before issuance merge this protocol and generator into main; no real forecast bytes in this PR. Then separate public run produces a candidate artifact from pinned sources, and a public commit records its exact SHA256, run evidence, actual first-public time, protocol commit and source hashes. No claim of issuance until evidence verified. Gate the run clock before and after computation and the later publication timestamp before the deadline. Forecast JSON is deterministic (no changing clock fields). Do not change issued bytes or any of the prior 620 forecasts. Forecast publication is experimental, not demonstrated skill.
Resolve using first annual GED release covering each calendar event year: 27.1 for 2026, 28.1 for 2027, after confirming official version and coverage and pinning original ZIP hashes. 30-day outcome only uses GED27.1; 90-day combines per-year counts with full window containment, deduplicate within each annual source and fail on cross-year eligible duplicate IDs. No partial scoring, no Candidate provisional substitution. Require full universe and frozen eligibility, binary outcomes and proper scores as in previous benchmark, report every model, and tie-aware operational metrics. A separate resolver must be implemented and tested before scoring. No lead-time claim; feature cutoff is 31 days before target start and the actual publication time is recorded separately. Seasonal transfer (Jan–Mar to Aug–Oct, May to winter), 2024 quarantine and sparse historical positives remain major limitations.
