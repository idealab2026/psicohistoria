# FIN-01B monthly realized volatility — protocol v1 (2026-11-27)

Freeze before retrieving daily data or evaluating this endpoint. Target: calendar-month realized volatility, sqrt(sum of squared daily broad US market total returns), expressed as a monthly decimal, not annualized. It measures realized variation including daily jumps; not option-implied volatility or a direction forecast. Source: Kenneth French legacy December 2024 archive daily factors, https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/Ken.french/data_library_202412_archive.html ; original ZIP https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/Ken.french/ftp_202412/F-F_Research_Data_Factors_daily_CSV.zip . Daily return = (Mkt-RF + RF)/100. Require unique ordered valid dates, no missing factor sentinels, complete monthly coverage July 1926–December 2024 and sensible minimum observation count (>=15 trading days in test/training months from 1950). No filling gaps. Check daily coverage limitations separately; no claim to independent exchange-calendar verification. Archive hash and structural counts, aggregates only, no raw daily data redistribution.

Compare fixed forecasts of monthly variance v=sum(r_d^2), evaluate variance QLIKE and monthly volatility MAE (sqrt variance). Primary model equal-weight heterogeneous autoregressive proxy: arithmetic average of last observed monthly variance, trailing 3-month mean, and trailing 12-month mean. References: expanding historical mean since Jan 1950; trailing 12-month mean (primary reference); last observed month; exponentially weighted monthly variance, lambda .94, seeded first observed month. All models use exact t-2 cutoff, including in historical rolling evaluation. No parameter tuning, future-dependent scaling or annualization. Evaluate Jan 1990–Dec 2024 (420 targets). Report losses pooled, chronological slices 1990–2004/2005–2014/2015–2024 and monthly/yearly aggregate errors; no raw outcomes or daily values. QLIKE = v/p - log(v/p) - 1 (lower better); MAE = abs(sqrt(p)-sqrt(v)); no zero observations or forecasts admitted. Primary contrast HAR proxy vs trailing12 in QLIKE; report all other models regardless of sign. Results retrospective, modern archive, not authentic vintage, no prospective forecast or economic utility.
