# COUP2025 temporal extension — protocol before new target calculation (2026-11-27)

This is a retrospective, post-development temporal extension, not an as-of 2025 issue or genuinely independent prospective confirmation. The complete year 2025 is outside the 1980–2024 development/evaluation origins. The 2026-08-29 Powell–Thyne archive was already used to construct historical inputs; 2025 target rows have not been used in model development or the earlier scores. The author's page https://jonathanmpowell.com/coups/ explicitly warns that this version includes tentative coding and planned larger revisions. Do not claim final truth or certified completeness.

## Fixed endpoint and population
Use all 195 codes belonging to COW on 2024-12-31, from the SHA-pinned States2024/System2024 archives; reproduce the frozen roster audit first. Define a distinct operational label: at least one overt failed or successful event recorded with that exact COW code and a valid date in calendar 2025 in the pinned Powell–Thyne CSV (coup 1 or 2). A 0 means no matching coded event in that edition, not verified absence of a real coup or verified ongoing sovereignty. No silent name matching, no adding 2025 entrants, no pretending this is Jan1 2025 COW membership. Report outside-roster events and missing/invalid codes or dates as blockers requiring adjudication; abort on invalid or duplicate rows or outside-roster event codes. The endpoint is a source-record indicator, separate from the strict historical in-membership endpoint. A full scientific comparison requires documenting this estimand difference, including competing exit. No future-year target is generated.

## Fixed evaluation
Fit the already frozen rules using the original strict country-year panel through 2023 only; recent climatology uses 2019–2023. Reuse original shrinkage k=20, recurrence feature y-6..y-2, and the same mean constraint across the entire fixed roster. Preserve all 195 test codes. Dummy test labels passed to forecast must never enter fitting; validate forecast invariance under changing all dummy labels. Evaluate recent climatology, calibrated country-history and calibrated recurrence; report Brier, log loss, positive count, mean predicted probability and tie-aware top 5/10/20 expected alert hits. Primary descriptive contrast recurrence vs recent (fixed before scoring); country vs recent mandatory secondary. No fitting/tuning on 2025, no using 2024/2025 observations as predictors. Do not treat 195 dependent country observations or a single year as independent evidence of skill. Freeze aggregate-only results and archive hashes.

## Prospective issuance
The experiment is not permission to issue COUP2027. Before issuance, reconcile source semantics, 2025 coverage, status of 195 codes, alternative endpoint resolution and missing-label policy; pin a complete training archive published before issue. Publish a protocol and immutable candidate before the target begins. Retain all three fixed models. Do not adjust them based on the 2025 result. No new or altered forecasts in this PR.
