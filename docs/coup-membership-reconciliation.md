# COUP event / membership reconciliation (pre-outcome protocol)

2026-11-26. Exact Powell–Thyne August CSV and COW v2024 ZIP SHA pins from the two frozen census JSONs; no current download substituted. Source author explicitly documents coup 1=failed and 2=successful (https://jonathanmpowell.com/coups/). Both included; year 1950–2024 only. Use numeric ccode (COW), never ccode_gw, polity code or name. Audit missing/invalid codes, dates (including unknown month/day), unexpected coup values, exact duplicate rows and potential duplicate same-country/date keys. Do not silently deduplicate ambiguous records or assert that unmatched rows represent negative outcomes. Exclude out-of-scope years from reconciliation but count them. No per-event details in report.

Construct interval membership (inclusive start/end) and compare year-panel keys with any-day overlap, Jan 1, July 1, Dec 31 and full-calendar-year continuity; report differences in both directions. Audit overlaps/reentries and count origin-member states exiting/interrupted during target year, and states entering after Jan 1. Join only exact dates within a membership interval; count failures by reason (missing code, unknown code, invalid date, date outside interval, missing system-year key). Aggregate unique positive country-years and counts of events by outcome coding among matched rows, and separately origin members and full-year members. No model or final negative labels yet. If nonmatches or ambiguous dates/duplicate keys exist, require separate source reconciliation or explicit exclusions with sensitivity analysis before backtest. The primary future estimand must distinguish a fixed Jan-1 risk set from selection on observed full-year membership (potential survivorship bias). No new forecasts.
